Privacy Policy

Zuporbi: Spiral Drop (Android game)

Effective date: 13 October 2026

1. Summary

  • The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your phone. We run no servers that receive it.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads are requested as child-directed, with the lowest content rating (G). Google handles the data that ads need, as described in section 5.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not use your data for our own tracking, profiling or analytics.

2. Who we are

"Zuporbi: Spiral Drop" is published by Veenslay ("we", "us", "the developer"). For any question about this policy or about privacy in the game, write to veenslay.np@gmail.com.

3. Information stored on your phone

To let you continue where you stopped, the game saves a small file on your phone (inside the game's private storage). It contains:

  • your progress: levels finished, stars, coins, experience points, chests, daily missions, streak and badges;
  • your choices: settings (sound, vibration, swipe speed, colour help, low effects), the ball, trail, splash mark and tower theme you picked, and what you bought with in-game coins;
  • your player profile: a name and a face made from shapes. The name is either made up by the game or typed by you. Please do not use your real full name or any personal details;
  • towers you made in the Tower Maker and towers you received from friends, with the results;
  • simple counters used for badges and missions, and a counter used to decide when a between-level ad may appear.

This file stays on your phone. We cannot read it. It is removed when you uninstall the game or use Settings > Reset progress.

4. Features that touch other apps

Backup code

On the Profile page you can create a backup code, a long piece of text that holds your progress. It is only created when you tap the button, and it is copied to your clipboard. Where you keep or send it is up to you. Anyone who has the code can restore that progress, so keep it private.

Sharing towers and results

When you tap Send or Share, the game prepares a message (for example a tower code or your profile card with your player name, level and stars), copies it to the clipboard and may open WhatsApp or another app so you can send it. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it. After that, the other app's own privacy policy applies.

Clipboard

When the game opens, it reads the text on your clipboard on your phone only, to check whether it contains a friend's tower code, and if so it offers to play it. The text is not stored and is not sent anywhere. Android may show you a short notice when an app reads the clipboard.

5. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC). There are two kinds:

  • Rewarded ads: shown only when you choose them, for example to continue after a fall, to double the coins from a level, or to get an extra try in the Daily Tower;
  • Between-level ads: shown now and then after a finished level, never straight after a fall, and never to a player who bought Remove ads.

Because children may play this game, every ad request is marked as child-directed and for users under the age of consent, and ad content is limited to the G (General Audiences) rating. In this mode, ads are not personalised.

To load and show an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google Play services may collect and use technical information from your device, such as your IP address, device and operating system details, app information, and identifiers such as the advertising ID, together with ad interaction and diagnostic data. This is done by Google for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and security, under Google's own policies. We do not receive your personal information from this process and we do not combine it with anything else.

Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your phone's Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads; the exact menu depends on your phone).

6. Purchases

Where offered, the game may sell an optional one-time purchase called Remove ads through Google Play Billing. Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see or store your card or bank details. The game only learns that the purchase was made, and keeps that on your phone. Restore purchases in Settings asks Google Play whether you own it, for example on a new phone.

8. Children

The game is suitable for a general audience, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and the game has no accounts, no open chat and no way to contact other players inside the game. Ads are requested in child-directed mode with a G content rating. A player name can be typed, so we ask players, and parents of younger players, not to enter real names or other personal details.

If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, write to veenslay.np@gmail.com.

9. Data we collect and share

The developer does not operate any server for this game, and does not run its own analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools. Because of this:

  • we collect no personal information through the game;
  • we share no data with third parties, other than the data that Google's ad and billing software handles on its own behalf as described in sections 5 and 6;
  • we do not sell personal data.

Google Play may give us general, anonymous statistics about installs and crashes through Google Play Console. They do not identify you.

10. Security and retention

Your save file sits in the game's private storage on your phone. We do not keep copies. To delete all game data, use Settings > Reset progress or uninstall the game. If you created a backup code, the code lives wherever you saved it, so delete it from there.

11. Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to look up or delete on our side. You can still write to us with any request about privacy. For data handled by Google's ad software, you can use Google's own tools and your phone's advertising settings, and Google's policies describe your rights.

12. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy, for example by adding a feature that collects data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change is released. Please check it from time to time.
